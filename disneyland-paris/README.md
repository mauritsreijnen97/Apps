# Our Magic Weekend

A personal trip companion for Max, Marjo & Maurits: Disneyland Hotel, Sunday 29 – Monday 30 November 2026.
Personal planner, not an official Disney app. No prices anywhere.

**Features:** live countdown with an animated night sky (castle, fireworks, snow) · two-day itinerary that
highlights what's next during the trip · 28 attractions with per-person votes, "ridden" tracking and a
vote-weighted ride roulette · tap-to-explore resort map · restaurant wish list and booking tracker ·
hotel details, packing list and tips · photo album and Buzz Lightyear / Spider-Man score battle · achievements.

Everything is saved on the device (localStorage); nothing is sent anywhere.

## Use it on the iPad
Open `index.html` in Safari (e.g. via GitHub Pages) → Share → **Add to Home Screen** for a full-screen app.

## Files
- `app.html` – the app source (body fragment, also published as a claude.ai artifact)
- `index.html` – standalone page generated from `app.html` with home-screen metadata
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` – home-screen app assets
