# Our Magic Weekend

A personal information guide for Max, Marjo, Martina & Maurits: Disneyland Hotel, Sunday 29 – Monday 30 November 2026.
Personal guide, not an official Disney app. No prices.

**What's in it:** personal greeting for each of you · live countdown over a photo hero with fireworks and snow ·
your Extra Magic Time (08:00–09:00 both mornings) and park hours for your two dates · Christmas season shows
(Mickey's Dazzling Christmas Parade 15:55 & 17:45, Disney Tales of Magic, Disney Cascade of Lights, A Celebration in Arendelle) ·
28 attractions with photos, magic-hours badges, filters and detail sheets · resort map · restaurants and what your booking includes ·
your Deluxe room and perks · sources for every researched fact in the credits sheet.

Hours and show times come from published 2026 schedules and guides and can change: check the official app on the day.

## Photos
The private version (published as a claude.ai artifact) also uses your own photos. **They are deliberately not in this public
repository.** The repo copy uses freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons, credited in the app.

## Files
- `app.html` – the app (body fragment, as published)
- `index.html` – standalone page generated from `app.html` with home-screen metadata
- `photos/` – Wikimedia Commons photos (see in-app credits)
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` – home-screen app assets
