Coming up today

  1. General strike: your lines run, central Paris does not

    Transilien forecasts normal service on lines L and U, and RER A, B, C and E run normally. From 11 AM, 17 stations close and metro 6 (Étoile to Kléber) and 10 (Austerlitz to Jussieu) are cut; the march leaves Nation at 2 PM for Place d'Italie, with 40,000 to 80,000 expected. Schools and public services are the most affected.

  3. GHR hotel and restaurant congress at Paris Bercy

    The national GHR 2026 congress for hoteliers and restaurateurs opens today in Bercy. Also on the agenda: Iliad Q3 results at 8 AM and Alstom half-year results after the close.

Worth knowing

  1. Amadeus breach list keeps growing

    Caribbean Airlines joined Singapore, Cathay, ANA, JAL, Korean, Malaysia and Condor among carriers whose booking data was exposed via an Amadeus display system, with access running from August 2023 to September 2026. Qantas is investigating.

  2. Airbnb pushes into hotels

    Airbnb is adding hotels to take on Expedia and Booking.com, and says AI already handles about 40% of its customer service.

  3. Expedia raises its bookings outlook

    After a Q3 beat, Expedia lifted full-year booking growth to about 10%, citing B2B growth and AI efficiencies, and argues running live inventory and payments protects it from AI agents.

  4. What today's strike may cost

    Estimates put the cost of today's six-union strike at 300 million to 1 billion euros, landing four weeks before the Christmas retail peak.
